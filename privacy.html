Privacy notice

Last updated October 29, 2026. Invoice Studio is operated as a Bahamas AI Solutions product.

Information used

Account details include your email address, name and optional username. Each business workspace stores the customer, billing, service, financial and contact information its authorized members enter. Passwords and Google sign-in are handled by our identity provider, not stored by this website.

Why we use it

To authenticate members, maintain their business workspaces, create printable invoices and management reports, and support the service. Business data is visible to members authorized by the workspace administrator. Avoid uploading card numbers, government IDs or sensitive medical information.

Service providers

The app requires hosted authentication and database infrastructure from Supabase. Static website hosting is intended for Cloudflare Pages. Google may be used if you choose Google sign-in. These providers may process account and technical data to deliver the service.

Data control and retention

Authorized users can export workspace records as JSON/CSV. Deleting browser storage does not remove cloud data. Account and workspace deletion require an operator-assisted process until self-service deletion is implemented. Independent backups and retention procedures need to be finalized before public launch.

Requests

For access, correction or deletion requests, contact us through Bahamas AI Solutions. We do not claim that this draft notice replaces legal review for the countries where the app will operate.
